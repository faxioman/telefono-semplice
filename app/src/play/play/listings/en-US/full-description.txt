Simple Phone turns an Android smartphone into a very easy phone, designed for elderly people or people living with dementia, and for the family members who look after them.

One single screen that never changes: the time and date in large print and the photos of loved ones. Tap a photo and the call starts, already on speakerphone.

WHAT IT DOES
• Replaces the home screen and the phone app: no icons, menus or apps to get lost in
• Contacts with big photos: one tap to call (accidental double taps are ignored)
• Speakerphone always on, for outgoing and incoming calls
• Full-screen incoming call: photo, name and two huge buttons, "Answer" and "Hang up"
• If a call is already in progress, a second caller gets a busy signal
• Call blocking: blacklist, or only contacts and allowed numbers; block private numbers
• Big numeric keypad, which can be turned off
• Screen always on and adjustable brightness; the power button can be ignored
• Light or dark theme, portrait and landscape
• 10 languages: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi and Chinese

FOR THE FAMILY
All settings sit behind a small PIN-protected icon: contacts and photos (also from the address book), missed and blocked calls with a "Call back" button, quick access to messages, language, theme and screen.

PRIVACY
The app does not use the Internet, has no ads and collects no data: contacts, photos and settings stay on the phone.

To work, Simple Phone must be set as the default phone app and as the home screen: the guided setup is in the settings.
